2 | PRESSURE DEFINITION & MATCH-STATE RECONSTRUCTION
Pre-delivery chase state; outcome of the current delivery is never used to label its own state
COHORT Regulation 2nd innings; match has innings 1 and 2; full 20-over allocation; exclude abandoned/no-result, DLS/revised target/overs, and any match with Super
Over.
PRE-DELIVERY
SCORE
score_before = cumulative sum of (batter runs + extras1 + extras2) before this delivery.
STATE R = target − score_before; B = 120 − legal balls used; W = 10 − wickets lost. Target = reconstructed 1st-innings team total + 1.
RATES RRR = 6 × R / B (when R>0 and B>0); opening RRR = 6 × target / 120.
PRESSURE FLAG Primary: RRR − opening RRR > 0. Positive means the chase has fallen behind its initial required pace. It is a transparent state marker, not psychological
pressure, win probability, or proof of clutch ability.
BOUNDARY
CONDITIONS
Exclude states after the target, after 120 legal balls, and after an unresolved 'other' extra has already appeared. An 'other' event is retained only at its pre-delivery
state; bowling outcome rows tagged 'other' are omitted from the primary bowling composite.
Both competitions use the same arithmetic, but states and player rates are reconstructed in separate cohorts.

3 | SCORE, ELIGIBILITY & FAIR-COMPARISON AUDIT
The ranking is an opportunity-gated, cohort-relative descriptive screen
BATTERS | Composite = ( z(shrunk pressure strike rate) + z(shrunk pressure boundary-run share) + z(−shrunk pressure dot rate) ) ÷ 3
Pressure batter runs use the delivery batter-runs field; batter balls exclude wides and include no-balls. Boundary-run share uses batter boundary runs / batter runs; dot rate uses
pressure balls with zero team runs / balls.
Batter shrinkage: SR and dot rate use 30 prior pressure balls at the pressure-cohort mean; boundary-run share uses 300 prior batter runs. Eligibility: at least 30 pressure balls across
at least 5 pressure innings.
BOWLERS | Composite = ( z(−shrunk pressure economy) + z(shrunk bowler wickets per 100 legal balls) + z(shrunk legal-dot rate) ) ÷ 3
Bowler runs include batter runs plus recorded wide/no-ball penalty extras; exclude byes/leg-byes and omit 'other'-tagged delivery outcomes. Legal balls exclude wides and Overstep.
Bowler-credit wickets: Caught, Bowled, LBW, Stump, Hit Wicket; run-outs and other non-credit dismissals are not credited.
Bowler shrinkage uses 60 prior legal balls at cohort means; eligibility: at least 60 classified pressure legal balls across at least 5 pressure innings.
Opportunity fairness: gates, shrinkage and displayed raw workload reduce small-sample dominance; z-scores standardize eligible players within their own competition cohort. Equal
weights are transparent but normative and do not make the composite an objective talent estimate.
Rank order is descending composite; ties break on more pressure balls, then more pressure runs (batters) or wickets (bowlers). Sensitivity is reported on page 7.

10 | DIRECT ANSWERS, LIMITATIONS & HUMAN REVIEW
Competition-readiness verdict against the Round 1 aim

Overall comparison? Each IPL top-10 batter has overall and pressure strike rate; each bowler has overall and pressure economy, plus pressure workload. The audit table puts
those side by side.
Reliable under pressure? This pipeline ranks pressure output, not demonstrated reliability/persistence. A same-player pressure-vs-overall contrast and sensitivity are supplied; no
temporal/repeated-split reliability validation exists.
Auction actions? The six files lack official 2027 auction eligibility/availability, current RR retained roster, purse, prices, roster slots and role needs. Therefore player names
remain evidence screens, not verified auction targets.
